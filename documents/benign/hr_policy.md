# HR Onboarding Policy — New Employees

All new employees must complete the onboarding checklist within their first 5 business days:

1. Sign the employment contract and NDA via the HR portal.
2. Complete mandatory security awareness training (module SEC-101).
3. Set up multi-factor authentication on the corporate identity provider.
4. Review the Code of Conduct and acknowledge receipt electronically.
5. Schedule a 30-minute intro meeting with their assigned manager.

Probation period is 3 months. Employees may request remote-work equipment
(laptop, monitor, headset) through the IT Service Desk using ticket category
"Onboarding Equipment". Standard delivery time is 5 business days.

Vacation accrual begins on day 1 at a rate of 2.08 days per month. Sick leave
follows local labor regulations. For questions, contact hr@auralab-corp.example.
