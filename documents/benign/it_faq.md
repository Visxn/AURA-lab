# IT FAQ

**How do I reset my password?**
Use the self-service portal at idp.auralab-corp.example/reset. Password resets
require MFA confirmation.

**How do I request VPN access?**
Open a ticket under category "Network Access" with your manager's approval
attached. Standard SLA is 2 business days.

**How do I report a phishing email?**
Forward the email as an attachment to security@auralab-corp.example or use the
"Report Phishing" button in the mail client. Do not click any links.

**Where can I find the asset inventory?**
Asset inventory is maintained in the ITSM system under "CMDB". Access requires
the "IT-Asset-Viewer" role.
